KoShelf

KoShelf has not been notarized by Apple.
Download and use it at your own risk.

I built KoShelf for fun with AI coding tools and guided the work myself.
It's a personal project I'm sharing with friends. Testing and review have
been limited. KoShelf can move and modify files on your Mac and Kindle,
so keep a separate backup of your library before using it.

The app uses an ad hoc signature. macOS may show a Gatekeeper warning
the first time you open it.
