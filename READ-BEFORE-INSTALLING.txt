KoShelf

KoShelf has not been notarized by Apple.
Download and use it at your own risk.

I built KoShelf for fun with AI coding tools and guided the work myself.
It's a personal project I'm sharing with friends. Testing and review have
been limited. KoShelf can move and modify files on your Mac and Kindle,
so keep a separate backup of your library before using it.

KoShelf has been tested on Apple silicon Macs only. The Intel build is
included but untested.

The app is signed with the developer's own certificate, not an Apple
Developer ID, so macOS may show a Gatekeeper warning the first time you
open it. Apple Books features need Full Disk Access; later KoShelf
updates signed with the same certificate keep that permission.

To open it the first time, Control-click KoShelf and choose Open.
Don't turn off Gatekeeper for your whole Mac.

If someone sent you this disk image, compare its checksum with the one
listed on the KoShelf release page before opening it:

    shasum -a 256 KoShelf-<version>-macOS.dmg
