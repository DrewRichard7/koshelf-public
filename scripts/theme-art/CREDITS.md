# Theme art credits

Art in this folder is tinted and inlined into `static/themes.css` by `scripts/build-themes.py`. All of it was made for KoShelf and is released under CC0 1.0.

| File | Source |
| --- | --- |

The Everforest forest is inspired by the misty spruce wallpaper in Omarchy's Everforest theme. It is drawn from scratch and contains nothing from that photo.
